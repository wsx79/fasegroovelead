Fase Groove Lead is installed!

VST3 PLUG-IN
- Open your DAW and rescan the plug-ins if it doesn't find it by itself.
- Load "Fase Groove Lead" as an INATTACKENT on a MIDI / instrument track.
- Installed in: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Fase Groove Lead.vst3

STANDALONE APP
- Start it from the Start menu. There is no DAW: press PLAY (or the space bar) to hear the grooves.

FIRST STEPS
1. Pick a groove (click the groove's name: the groove browser opens).
2. Press PLAY.
3. Pick the chords (CHORDS panel), or hold a chord on the MIDI input with MIDI IN on.
4. Drag the orange "DRAG MIDI TO DAW" tile onto a lead (or keys) track.

User guide: docs\GUIDE.md in the installation folder - https://github.com/wsx79/fasegroovelead
