# Privacy – Fase Groove Lead

*Last updated: 19 October 2026 · 🇮🇹 [Versione italiana](PRIVACY.it.md)*

Fase Groove Lead respects your privacy in the simplest possible way: **it collects nothing**.

- **No personal data** is collected, stored or sent.
- **No analytics, telemetry, ads or license checks.**
- **Optional update check.** On opening, once per session, the plug-in asks GitHub for updates and may download a newer build. GitHub receives the request’s IP address and "FaseGrooveLead-Updater" user agent. No grid, MIDI notes, samples or projects are uploaded. Automatic checking is enabled by default and can be disabled in **OPTIONS → SETTINGS**; manual checking remains available.
- **No account** and no registration.

## What stays on your computer

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plug-in settings, your grid and the paths of your samples | Inside your DAW project | So you get them back when you reopen the project (saved by the DAW). |
| Dragged MIDI clips | `Documents/Fase Groove Lead/MIDI clips/` | Dragging to the DAW needs a real file. You can delete them at any time. |
| `.mid` files saved with *SAVE .MID* | Wherever you choose | At your request. |
| Interface language | `Fase Groove Lead.settings` in your system's user settings folder | So the plug-in keeps your language in every project. |
| Audio/MIDI settings of the Standalone app | Your system's user settings folder | Handled by the JUCE framework to remember your audio interface. |
